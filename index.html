Selected work

  • Autonomous Dog Ball Thrower

    100+ m of adaptive throws per session. The launcher reads how tired the dog is, adjusts each throw, and logs the session to a live dashboard. See the project

  • Grip Gadget

    A 2-DOF forearm-mounted grip-assist wearable that reaches 30″ and grips a trash bag with no finger movement. Co-designed with Tony, a UC Berkeley facilities worker, and cut 40% in weight. See the project

  • Autonomous Sail Drone

    Built and assembled from scratch, then improved through CAD iterations: 25% lighter, rebuilt for solar, and validated at the marina sailing in all 8 directions, upwind included. See the project

  • Canine Thoracic Limb Prosthesis

    A fitted prosthetic leg for Ollie, a senior dog. Reshaped through four fittings on the dog himself, guided by his owner at every step. See the project

  • EnableTech Website

    An accessibility-first site built to WCAG 2.1 AA. Keyboard-operable, screen-reader friendly, and designed for everyone from the start. See the project
